# 86. The page that was given out · ✅ Done · S

Asked for from the sofa: *Now quiet ne alaka? Now için daha iyi bir
isimlendirme bulamaz mıyız? Buraya yeni menü ile ne kadar ihtiyaç kaldı, hala
tutmalı mıyız* - what has "Quiet" got to do with anything, is there a better
name for `Now`, and now that the quick menu exists, is the page still needed?
Then: *kaldıralım, önerdiğin gibi dağıt, space lock ve keep controller bence
spaces altında yer alsın* - remove it, give its tiles out, and put the
workspace lock and *Keep the controller* under `Spaces`.

**`Quiet` was a fallback nobody wrote as a word.** The nav card printed the
title of what was playing and `Quiet` when nothing was; on its own, under a
card called `Now`, it read as a mode rather than as silence.

**And the page had lost what made it the first one.** `Now` was the keyboard,
the volume, what was playing, the transport, the stopwatch, and - while there
was anything to use them on - the workspace lock and its pair. It came first
because it held what somebody reaches for without having decided anything.
[85](85-two-menus-two-buttons.md) gave that job its own button: the quick menu
on `PLUS` has the keyboard and the volume in one row. What was left on `Now`
was sound, a stopwatch, and two tiles about who the presses go to - three
errands under a name that named none of them. The mockup's `Now` is the
session that is running, with `RESUME` under it, and that is `Resume` in the
quick menu too.

**So each tile went to the page of its errand:**

| Tile | Went to | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Music, Previous, Next, Volume, Mute | `Sound`, above the device cards | What is playing is a question about sound. `Audio` was renamed `Sound` with it: the page is what you hear now, not only where it goes |
| Workspace lock, Keep the controller | `Spaces`, under the window card | The lock holds the pad to the game on this workspace; keeping the controller is its other side |
| Start here | `Controller`, first | Everything on its page is about the pad. It still opens the menu on itself the first time (`open_on`) |
| Stopwatch | `System`, last | It belongs to no errand, and System is the page of the machine rather than of anything on it |
| Keyboard | nowhere | The quick menu has it, and the keyboard's own binding opens it |

`Sound`'s nav card prints the title when something plays and the speakers
when nothing does, which says *whether* and *where* in one line - and its
fallback is a device's name, not a mood.

The bar opens on `Apps` now, which is where a console's home opens: on what
there is to start.

**Rejected:** keeping the page under a better name - `Playing`, `Media`.
Without the keyboard and the lock tiles it would have been the top half of
`Sound` on a page of its own, one shoulder away from the bottom half.
