# Research

Documents written outside this project, kept here because an argument in
`components/` or `roadmap.md` leans on them.

| Document | What it surveys |
|---|---|
| [`console-launcher-ux.md`](console-launcher-ux.md) | What the four console shells do with a pad on a television, and the focus rules tvOS documents. September 2026. |

Three rules, and they are what keep this folder from becoming a second
`conventions/`:

1. **Nothing here is normative.** A rule this project keeps is written in
   [`../conventions/`](../conventions/), in this project's own words, with the
   reason attached. Citing a survey is not the same as adopting it.
2. **A source is kept as it was received** - its spelling, its numbers, its
   opinions - with a header saying where it came from and when. Anything else
   is a paraphrase wearing a citation.
3. **It is dated, or it is not evidence.** Every platform in here ships four
   times a year. A survey with no date cannot be told from a memory.

What earns a file: something outside this tree that a decision inside it was
actually made against. What does not: a link worth reading once - that belongs
in the change that read it.
